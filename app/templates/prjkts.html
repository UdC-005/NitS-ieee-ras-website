Soft Actor-Critic Based Adaptive PID Control for Energy-Efficient Legged Robot Locomotion

Frames legged locomotion as a Markov Decision Process and uses a Soft Actor-Critic agent to tune PID gains in real time. Simulations on a quadruped robot report 20–25% lower power consumption without compromising trajectory tracking accuracy or gait stability.

Reinforcement learningControl systems

Presented at the 11th Indian Control Conference (ICC 2025), IISc Bengaluru.
